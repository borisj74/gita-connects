Inside the canvas

Study the Gita as a web

  1. 1

    Start from any verse

    All 701 verses are ready, each with a short reading, its theme and its key concepts. No setup and no account.

  2. 2

    See everything it connects to

    One click fans out the verses it is linked to, from the very next verse to one sixteen chapters away. 1,057 curated links across the whole Gita.

  3. 3

    Know why they connect

    Every link carries a type, a strength and a one-line reason. Tap a label to read it. Ten link types, each in its own colour.

  4. 4

    Add your own reading

    Bring in any verse, drag from one to another to make your own link, and keep notes as you go. Everything saves by itself.

  5. 5

    See what your study says

    A live panel names the ideas your verses share, the verse holding them together and where to read next. Save it, export it or check it with AI.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Write to hello@gitaconnects.com.

Is it free?

Yes. The canvas, the curated links, notes and export are all free, and you don’t need an account. Sign in only if you want your work on more than one device.

Whose translation is it?

The translation and purports are from Bhagavad-gītā As It Is by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. The Sanskrit and transliteration come from a public-domain edition.

The themes, summaries and connections around them are Gita Connects’ own.

Who made the connections, and how far can I trust them?

The people behind Gita Connects curated all 1,057 of them. Each one has a type, a strength from 1 to 10 and a one-line reason you can read on the canvas.

Treat them as a reading, not the last word. Hide the link types you don’t need, draw your own, and if you sign in, run “Check with AI” on the links you made.

Do I need to know Sanskrit, or the Gita already?

No. Search in plain English by theme or concept, or open any chapter and start from a single verse. Every card carries a short summary and the ideas it teaches.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Everything works by touch: drag from the dot under one card to another to link them, and pinch to move around the canvas.

Can I share what I make?

Export any network as a PNG or PDF to send or print. You can also export your saved networks as a file and import them in another browser.
